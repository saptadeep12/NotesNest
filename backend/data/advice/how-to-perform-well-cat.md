Focus mostly on your classes. That's where the bulk of your marks come from.

Before an exam, spend your time on your weak topics instead of re-reading what you already know.

Take quizzes very seriously. They are very important for a good CGPA.

- **ChatGPT:** use it for summaries and MCQs, especially for soft skill subjects.
- **YouTube:** use it when you need any topic explained.
