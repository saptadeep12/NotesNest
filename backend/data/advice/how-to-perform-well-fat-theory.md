---
title: How to perform well in FAT theory?
category: Exams
summary: Revise instead of learning new topics, use past assignments, quizzes and PYQs, and solve what you know first.
order: 3
---
Revise before the exam. Don't start any new topics at the last minute.

For revision, go through your previous assignments, quizzes and PYQs.

During the paper, solve the questions you know first, then come back to the rest.

- **Gemini:** use it for complex topics and extra practice questions.
- **NotebookLM:** use it to make flashcards for theory.
